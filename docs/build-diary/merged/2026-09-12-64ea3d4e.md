## 2026-09-11 — Event posts become one arc
The per-event pre-event and post-event posts became a single two-part arc: the pre-event post now sets the table on the topic, the presenter's perspective, and its broader implications without resolving them, and the post-event post resolves that setup against what was actually said, agreed, and argued. The research brief also became canonical as one discrete Content Draft instead of being duplicated into the Event page, so it keeps its multi-event relations, outcome properties, and clean retrieval provenance.

## 2026-09-11 — Role scoring judges the job
The role scanner now scores a role on what its job description says the seat does rather than on its title, screening out pure people-management seats while keeping player-coaches who carry a book, enforcing a $200K compensation floor, treating Mid-Market at a top-tier company as a full fit, and reserving A-tier for scores of 85 and above so the tier discriminates again. Seven leadership roles that had scored A were re-verified against their job descriptions and demoted in the same pass.

## 2026-09-09 — Technical selling on record
The candidate source of truth now carries explicit evidence of selling technical products to technical buyers across three prior roles, so every resume, profile, and interview-prep artifact generated from it inherits that proof instead of rediscovering it.
