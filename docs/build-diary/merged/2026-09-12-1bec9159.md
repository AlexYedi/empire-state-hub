## 2026-09-12 — Rigor that watches itself
A graph of the repo's own skills, commands, hooks and decision records now rebuilds at the start and end of every session and reports any reference pointing at a file that no longer exists, so a routine rename surfaces on its own instead of waiting for someone to run a check. The rigor layer had only ever recorded what was invoked; this is the first piece of it that is triggered.

## 2026-09-12 — Reference check stops misfiring
The dangling-reference check that caps the build-quality judge's completeness score no longer mistakes path templates for missing files, and no longer hides real broken references that share a line with a template. A self-test now asserts the judge's checker and the graph's extractor agree, where that rule had been held only by a comment.

## 2026-09-12 — The inbox as a signal source
Gmail became a first-class market-intelligence source: an allowlisted scan extracts company and product signals from newsletters into the graph, deduplicated on the redirect-resolved article URL so one story cited by four newsletters lands once. Resolving those links also caught two factual errors before they could reach content.
